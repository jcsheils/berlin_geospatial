## Decisions before substantive thesis inference

1. **Approve the October scope.** Retire the August watering-to-temperature hypothesis from the active plan. Do not describe the existing equity screening map as an allocation model.
2. **Define the daily population.** Construct complete eligible area-days; audit zeros and platform coverage; specify the street-tree age 5–19 offset and age-at-event convention. Current cadastre counts alone are not a historical risk set.
3. **Complete daily controls.** Audit Tmax and precipitation, calculate antecedent dry spells, and add heat warnings, holidays, age composition and a documented turnout crosswalk. The single DWD station cannot identify neighbourhood weather variation.
4. **Resolve the local LST source.** Request source boundaries, Buckow component identifiers, count semantics, scene dates, cloud filtering, compositing, rainfall provenance, and redistribution terms. The 93-area monthly comparisons remain provisional.
5. **Pre-register honestly.** Record which preliminary outcomes are already known. Lock prospective model and validation decisions before further outcome-driven tuning; a previously inspected period is not an untouched hold-out.
6. **Validate before ranking.** Require spatial and temporal validation, calibration, coverage sensitivity, and uncertainty before presenting area priorities. Need-only versus complement-based allocation involves an explicit policy choice.
