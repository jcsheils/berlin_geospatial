## Decisions before inference

1. **Approve the October scope.** Drop the August watering-to-temperature hypothesis, and do not present the equity screening map as an allocation model.
2. **Define the daily population.** Build complete eligible area-days, audit zeros and platform coverage, and specify the street-tree offset for ages 5–19 and the age-at-event convention. Current cadastre counts are not a historical risk set.
3. **Complete daily controls.** Audit Tmax and precipitation, compute antecedent dry spells, and add heat warnings, holidays, age composition and a documented turnout crosswalk. One DWD station cannot capture neighbourhood weather.
4. **Resolve the local LST source.** Request source boundaries, Buckow component IDs, count meaning, scene dates, cloud filtering, compositing, rainfall provenance and redistribution terms. Until then the 93-area comparisons stay provisional.
5. **Preregister honestly.** Record which outcomes are already known and lock model and validation choices before further tuning. An inspected period is not an untouched hold-out.
6. **Validate before ranking.** Require spatial and temporal validation, calibration, coverage sensitivity and uncertainty before ranking areas. Choosing between need-only and complement-based allocation is a policy decision.
