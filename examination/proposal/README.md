## Qualifications

- **Counts (Section 6.2).** The canonical May 2020–December 2025 sample, linked and assigned to an LOR, has **11,127 distinct trees**, **57,786 events** and **2,249,502 litres**. The full snapshot runs to April 2026 and has 11,278 tree IDs, 11,160 of which match the inventory. These are different samples. The proposal's alternatives of 11,087 and 11,245 come from unresolved filters and are not canonical. See the [reconciliation](../preliminary_results.md#sample-reconciliation).
- **Coordinates.** The full-snapshot audit flags 125 records as invalid or outside Berlin, but the canonical panel links by tree ID and uses inventory geometry, so that flag is not its exclusion rule. The canonical panel loses 688 unmatched or unassigned events.
- **Preregistration.** The monthly, spatial and equity outcomes have been examined. Any registration must disclose this and separate prospective hypotheses, specifications and held-out evaluation from retrospective exploration. No registration exists yet.
- **Novelty and citations.** The proposal's "first" and "no published method" statements still need a literature check. The heat-mortality citation listed as Burkart et al. should read Schuster, Burkart and Lakes (2014), as the [literature notes](../../docs/related_literature.md) record.
- **Appendix A.** The watering-density and detectability calculations are design rationale, not an experiment reproduced here. Read its "not recoverable" conclusion as a scoped feasibility judgement, not a proof for every design.
- **Daily feasibility.** The tree-day table holds only days with recorded activity. The balanced area-day panel, historical young-tree denominator, participation controls and hierarchical fits are planned. Many 2024–2025 timestamps sit at midnight UTC, so sub-daily timing is lost.
- **Data availability.** "All data are public" describes the intended design. Several inputs are still missing, and the supplied Ortsteil LST file lacks extraction metadata and a redistribution licence. See the [data guide](../../data/README.md).
