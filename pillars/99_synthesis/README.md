# Three-Part Synthesis and Care-Gap Atlas

**Readiness: blocked-planned**

## Role

Synthesis combines published outputs from Parts I–III into a coherent account
of heat exposure, vegetation, recorded citizen care, and spatial vulnerability.
It does not recreate upstream calculations inside a final notebook.

## Required inputs

- Part III component relationships, MAUP analysis, denominator sensitivity, and
  care-gap outputs;
- either the validated Part I thermal layer or the declared Environmental
  Justice Atlas fallback;
- the Part II detectability result and, only if the gate passes, its qualified
  association evidence as a separate layer.

## Planned outputs

- Berlin vulnerability and watering-equity atlas;
- calendar-month watering climatology, five year-specific January-December
  sheets, and an April-September comparison grid;
- part-to-part evidence matrix;
- sensitivity and uncertainty tables;
- final thesis figures and reproducibility manifest;
- policy recommendations proportionate to the evidence.

## Schedule protection

- At the end of Month 2, a missing analysis-ready Landsat/Sentinel panel freezes
  Part I at descriptive thermal context and activates the Environmental Justice
  Atlas thermal fallback.
- At the end of Month 3, protect the minimum Part III core before any
  conditional Part II modelling: Planungsraum analysis plus district
  sensitivity, three care measures, MSS/EJA components, denominator
  sensitivity, and the screening atlas.

## Integration rule

Every synthesis value must link to an upstream dataset ID, model specification,
and run manifest. Spatial joins preserve the coarsest source grain. Missing
population, thermal, municipal, or behavioural information remains visible as
missing and is not replaced with unlabelled assumptions.

Policy economics and heat-mortality modelling are future extensions, not
dependencies of the current synthesis.
