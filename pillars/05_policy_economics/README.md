# Future Extension — Policy and Economics

**Status: outside the active thesis scope**

The feedback-incorporated three-part proposal no longer includes a cost-benefit
chapter. This folder is retained only as a record of a possible later research
extension.

Any future economic analysis would require a defensible intervention-effect
estimate, municipal watering quantities and costs, transparent valuation of
volunteer time, energy or productivity response functions, and uncertainty
analysis. None of these inputs is required for thesis completion, the Part III
atlas, or the current synthesis.

No benefit-per-litre, welfare, productivity, or municipal cost comparison will
be reported in the active thesis without those dependencies.
