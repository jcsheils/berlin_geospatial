# Part I Support — Vegetation Greenness and Temporal Drought

**Readiness: blocked-planned**

## Role in the current thesis

This retained folder documents the vegetation–water mechanism and measurement
limits within Part I. It is no longer a standalone cooling-lag workstream.

## Current question

Is the adjusted association between vegetation greenness and Landsat LST weaker
during drier city-wide periods?

## Required inputs

- contemporaneous Sentinel-2 NDVI/vegetation greenness for each Landsat date;
- antecedent greenness variants for reverse-causality sensitivity;
- ESA CCI soil moisture and ERA5 moisture-balance variables as temporal
  modifiers;
- acquisition-day weather, cloud, season, and built-form controls.

## Removed objective

Planting-event and distributed-lag cooling-lag estimation are outside the
active thesis scope. They would require validated historical planting events
and repeated outcomes not currently available.

## Claim boundary

Sentinel-2 NDVI is not ready-made canopy cover. Coarse soil-moisture products
do not identify neighbourhood or root-zone moisture differences within Berlin.
The primary vegetation–LST analysis is associational.
